I am a PhD Candidate and Teaching Fellow in the Department of English Literature and Creative Writing at Queen’s University in Kingston, ON. I am also a Graduate Research Assistant on the [<I>Digital Victorian Periodical Poetry</i>](https://dvpp.uvic.ca/) project at the University of Victoria, where I received my MA (with a concentration in Nineteenth-Century Studies). I completed my BA (Hons, with distinction) at Toronto Metropolitan University (formerly Ryerson University), where I was a research assistant on [<i>The Yellow Nineties</i>](https://1890s.ca/).

My dissertation project examines the relationship between Irish and English literary cultures in the nineteenth-century periodical press, with particular attention to how Irish writers adapted their work for different publishing markets. More broadly, my research interests include nineteenth-century literature and culture, print culture, and the digital humanities. My work in digital humanities explores how computational methods and digital archives can facilitate new approaches to the study of nineteenth-century print culture.

My teaching and pedagogical work spans nineteenth-century literature, popular fiction, critical thinking, and academic writing. I have also developed curriculum for and coordinated the [Graduate Transition and Academic Success (GTAS) Program](https://www.queensu.ca/grad-postdoc/graduate-studies/international-students/graduate-transition-and-academic-success-program-gtas) at Queen’s University, a two-week program supporting incoming international graduate students as they transition to graduate study and life in Kingston.
